Posting with /space

  1. Go to the Brozo Discord General Chat channel.

  2. Type /space and fill in the Space or post URL, then pick or type a comment. Suggestions pop up as you reach the comment field — click one to use it, type your own, or pick [blank] for no comment.

  3. Review the preview — it shows the exact message and every server it will reach — then click Post.

Adding the announcements to your Discord

Your community can receive Spaces announcements without installing Zobot or any other bot — it works through a webhook you create and control.

  1. Click Edit Channel on the channel where you want the announcements.

  2. Open Integrations.

  3. Click Create Webhook. Name it whatever you like and make sure it points at the right channel.

  4. Click Copy Webhook URL and DM it to @ndrpxl.

That webhook URL works like a key to your channel — send it only by DM, never in a public channel.

Security

  • The webhook URL is a credential. Anybody holding it can post to that channel, so keep it secure.
  • You stay in control. Delete the webhook in your channel settings at any time and the announcements stop instantly.
  • The URL is never stored elsewhere. It lives only in Zobot’s configuration, nowhere else.
  • Posting is locked to named people. Only users authorized by their Discord ID can use /space.
  • Posts are X links only. Every post is a validated x.com link — nothing else gets through.
